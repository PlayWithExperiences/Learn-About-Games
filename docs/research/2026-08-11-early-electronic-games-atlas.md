## Claim boundary

The slice distinguishes experimental apparatus, experimental program, system prototype, commercial hardware, and game. Relations are only recorded when the source supports a directed transformation or design response. Same-year proximity is not an influence relation, and the Atlas makes no unconditional claim that Pong was the first video game or first commercial video game.
